Variance is the gap between what you should have used based on sales — theoretical usage — and what you actually used based on counts. That gap is waste, over-portioning, spoilage or theft. It is usually the single largest recoverable cost in an independent restaurant, and most operators never see it because nobody calculates it.

For most restaurants, 28–35% of revenue is healthy. Fine dining often runs higher (35–40%) because of premium ingredients; pizza and fast casual often run lower. The number that matters more than the benchmark is your own trend — a two-point creep month over month is the signal worth acting on.
