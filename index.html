The unfair advantage

Your stock counts itself.

Most apps just store a number you have to update by hand. ResBizAI actually knows how much you have left — because it watches what you sell and does the math for you.

1

Link a recipe, once

Tell ResBizAI what goes into each dish a single time — "Grilled Salmon = 1 fillet + 50g carrots." That's the only setup.

2

Record what sold

Enter the day's sales (or pull them from your POS) and every ingredient is subtracted automatically, down to the gram.

3

Count & catch leaks

A 30-second nightly count is compared to what the system expected — revealing exactly where waste, over-portioning, or theft is costing you.

A real example
You open with 50 salmon fillets. Tonight you sold 46 Grilled Salmon. ResBizAI instantly shows 4 left — nobody counted a thing. The next morning you count the shelf and find 3. That −1 gap is money leaking, and for the first time you can actually see it.
That gap between what you should have and what you actually have is called variance — the single most valuable number in a kitchen, and the one most restaurants never see.

ResBizAI is AI restaurant management software that handles staff scheduling, inventory, daily P&L, menu costing, and supplier orders — all through a simple chat or voice interface. Restaurant owners and managers run their entire operation by talking to it, like texting their smartest manager.

You link a recipe to each menu item once — the ingredients and amounts in every dish. When you record the day's sales (or connect your POS), ResBizAI automatically subtracts those ingredients from stock. Sell 46 grilled salmon and it knows 46 fillets are gone, so it shows what's left without anyone counting. A quick nightly count then reconciles the system against reality.

No. ResBizAI keeps a running, automatic estimate by subtracting recipes from your sales, so you always have a live number. A physical count is only needed periodically — usually once a night or once a week — to correct for waste and spillage and to reveal variance. The count takes about 30 seconds per section.

Variance is the difference between what the system expects you to have (based on sales) and what you actually count on the shelf. A negative gap means stock disappeared without a sale — waste, over-portioning, spoilage, or theft. It's the clearest signal of where a restaurant loses money, and ResBizAI surfaces it automatically every time you reconcile.
